Getting started

Finding your way around Tournament Guru.

Tournament Guru helps coaches, team managers, and parents find youth-sports tournaments worth traveling for. Every event page combines the details that matter — dates, location, age groups, competition level — with verified reviews from people who actually attended.

Event Directors claim and manage their own listings, so information comes straight from the source, and our team moderates the platform to keep it honest.

Head to Search Events and start from your city — you can filter by distance, dates, age group, gender, and competition level. Results are ranked with your location in mind.

Tip: set your travel distance under Account → Preferences and we'll prioritize events within range automatically.

Age groups follow the standard U8–U19 convention — a U12 bracket is for players aged 12 and under in that season. Competition level describes the strength of the field, from Highest (top club and showcase play) down to Lowest (recreational and developmental).

Directors set both on their listings, so you can match events to your team before you commit to the drive.

Reviews & ratings

How reviews are written, verified, and scored.

You can edit a review freely while it's pending. Once it's published, it becomes part of the event's permanent rating record — the score directors and other families rely on — so editing closes at that point.

If something in a published review is factually wrong (a typo'd venue, the wrong year), contact support and we'll set it right. You can always delete your own review from My Reviews.

Every review goes through the Guru check before it's published: our team confirms the reviewer actually attended the event — matching their team, age group, and dates against the tournament — and screens for spam or conflicts of interest.

Reviews that pass carry the Verified badge, and only verified reviews count toward an event's rating. That's the whole point of Tournament Guru: scores you can trust when you're deciding where to spend a weekend.

Each verified reviewer scores the event across four categories:

  • Organization — scheduling, communication, check-in
  • Facilities — fields, parking, amenities
  • Competition — quality and fairness of the bracket
  • Value — what you got for the entry fee and travel

An event's overall rating is the average across all verified reviews, and we show coach and parent scores separately — the two groups often experience the same weekend very differently.

Events & claiming

Listings, ownership, and Premium placement.

If your tournament is already listed, claiming it takes a few minutes:

  1. Sign up (or switch) to an Event Director account.
  2. Open the event page and choose “Claim this event.”
  3. Tell us your role and organization — our team verifies you're really the organizer.
  4. Once approved, the listing appears under your Events and you manage everything: details, dates, photos, and replies to reviews.

Most claims are reviewed within two business days. Not listed yet? Directors can add a new event from their dashboard instead.

Premium is a paid placement that directors purchase for a listing. Premium events appear in the Spotlight column, rank at the top of relevant searches, and get a richer page with banner imagery.

What Premium never changes: the reviews. Ratings are earned from verified attendees only — a Premium badge can't buy a better score.

Claimed listings are maintained by their directors, but mistakes happen. Use Contact support with a link to the event and what's off — dates, venue, fees — and we'll flag it to the director or correct unclaimed listings ourselves.

Account & privacy

Your data, your login, and what others can see.

No. The email you sign in with is never shown to anyone. Your reviews and comments display your first name and role (coach, parent, team manager) — nothing more.

Event Directors who want to be reachable set a separate public business contact on their profile; that's the only email that ever appears on the site, and it's theirs to choose.

Both live under Account → Security. Changing your email sends a confirmation link to the new address — the switch happens once you click it. Passwords need at least 8 characters with an uppercase letter and a number.

Locked out? Use Forgot password on the login screen and we'll email you a reset link.

Your personal data is removed, but published reviews and comments stay on the platform, anonymized as “Former member” — they're part of the rating history other families depend on.

If you'd rather take a review down entirely, delete it from My Reviews before deleting your account. Account deletion can't be undone.
