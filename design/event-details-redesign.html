About this tournament

The Kickoff Cup's flagship U12 girls bracket returns to Austin for its eighth year. Twenty-four teams across two competitive tiers play a guaranteed four-match weekend — group stage Saturday, semifinals and a third-place match Sunday morning, finals under the lights Sunday evening.

Every match is centrally refereed with certified USSF officials, rosters are verified at check-in, and standings post live to the tournament app. College and ODP scouts have attended the Upper bracket every year since 2022.

Teams travel from across Texas, Oklahoma and Louisiana; gate admission is free for spectators, and the complex offers free parking on both days.
