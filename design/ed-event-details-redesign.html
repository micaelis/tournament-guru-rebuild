About this event

Three days of competitive U10 soccer on the twelve grass fields of Onion Creek. Every team plays a four-game minimum with trophies for both finalists in each bracket. Certified referees on every field, athletic trainers on site all weekend, and a Friday-evening skills showcase to kick things off.
